# The Problem We're Solving

Our analytics show that 68% of users who add items to cart never complete checkout. That's leaving serious money on the table. Competitor analysis suggests the top friction point is payment flexibility — specifically, customers want to see their total cost upfront without getting surprised by fees at the final step.

# The Feature: "Pay Your Way" Checkout

I want us to build a checkout experience where users can toggle between payment options — credit card, PayPal, Klarna-style installments, or store credit — and see in real-time how each choice impacts their total, including any interest or fees. No surprises. Clean, transparent, builds trust.

## Key Requirements

Real-time price recalculation — subtotal, taxes, shipping, and financing costs update instantly as users switch options
Visual comparison — side-by-side or toggle view showing total cost of ownership for each method
Pre-qualification soft check — for installment options, give users an estimated rate without hard credit pull
Saved preferences — remember their last choice for next visit, reduce friction
Mobile-first — 73% of our traffic, so this needs to feel native on small screens
